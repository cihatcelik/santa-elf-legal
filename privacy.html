Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

Christmas Elf (“the app”, “we”, “us”) turns a photo you choose into a funny Christmas picture using AI. The app is operated by ELAPPS, Türkiye (the data controller). This policy explains what data the app uses, why, who processes it, how long it is kept, and how you can delete it.

In short
  • Your photo is used only to create the picture you asked for.
  • Your photo is sent to our AI image provider (Replicate, running Google’s Nano Banana model) for processing.
  • Your original photo and your results are stored privately in your account until you delete them.
  • We do not use your photos to train AI models, we do not sell your data, and the app has no advertising or tracking.
  • You can delete a single creation, or your whole account and all its data, at any time in the app.

2. AI processing of your photo

When you start a creation, the app gives our AI image provider Replicate temporary access (a private link valid for 15 minutes) to your photo. Replicate runs Google’s Nano Banana image model to create the picture; as the model provider, Google may also process the photo to produce the result. According to Replicate, inputs and outputs of these requests are deleted from Replicate after one hour; we copy the result to your private storage right away. Results are AI-generated and may not look exactly like you.

Before your first upload the app asks for your consent to this processing. If you do not agree, no photo is sent.

4. How long we keep data

  • Photos and results: until you delete the creation or your account.
  • Guest accounts (no email saved) that have not been used for 90 days and have no purchases are deleted automatically, together with their photos and results.
  • Purchase records: for as long as your account exists.
  • Abuse-protection records (hashed IP): 2 days.
  • Sign-in codes expire after one hour.

5. Deleting your data

  • One creation: open it and tap Delete. The original photo and the result are permanently deleted.
  • Everything: Settings → Delete my account and data. Your account, photos, results, credits (including purchased credits) and purchase history are permanently deleted, and we ask RevenueCat to delete its customer record.
  • You can also email us at elappstest@gmail.com.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or Türkiye’s KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your data protection authority. To use these rights, contact elappstest@gmail.com. We process your data to provide the service you request (contract), with your consent for AI processing of photos, and for our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. Please only upload photos of a child if you are the child’s parent or guardian or have their permission.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Photos and results are stored in private storage and shown only through short-lived links. Your sign-in session is kept in your device’s secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
